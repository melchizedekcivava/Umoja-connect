About Bright Umoja Connect

A grassroots civic-tech initiative founded by Eastlands residents to transform our courts, restore public amenities, and champion youth-led community development.

Our Story Meet the Team
Grassroots Origins

Built by the Residents, Powered by the Youth

Umoja Community Connect (popularly known as Bright Umoja) was born out of a shared frustration with overflowing garbage, chronic drainage blockages, and broken street lighting that compromised security in our estate.

In 2023, a coalition of court chairpersons, youth organizers, and tech creators decided that waiting for traditional bureaucracy was not enough. We launched a simple digital incident reporter paired with scheduled weekend cleanup drives. Today, the platform connects over 3,800 active households with Nairobi County field units, ensuring every neighborhood concern is tracked until it is verifiably resolved.

Our Compass

Mission, Vision & Core Values

The fundamental principles that guide our day-to-day engagement with residents and county leaders.

Our Mission

To empower every citizen in Umoja with transparent digital reporting tools, rapid civic escalation, and community-led initiatives that keep our estate clean, secure, and resilient.

Our Vision

A model urban community in Eastlands, Nairobi where public infrastructure functions reliably, green spaces thrive, and every young person has access to high-value skills and livelihoods.

Core Values

Transparency: Open public tracking on every ticket.
Civic Ownership: We protect our own neighborhood.
Youth Leadership: Mentoring the next generation.
Non-Partisan: Dedicated strictly to community well-being.

Our Milestones

How We Grew Over the Years

From a small WhatsApp emergency group to an officially recognized county community liaison platform.

Mid 2023

The Grassroots Awakening

Residents in Umoja 1 Court 18 joined hands with 25 youth to clear a 5-year-old illegal dump, recycling organic waste and inspiring surrounding courts.

Early 2024

Launch of Bright Umoja Platform

Digital ticket reporting system deployed. Within the first 6 months, 500+ reports were logged, reducing response times from 3 weeks to under 48 hours.

Late 2024

Nairobi County Recognition

Embakasi West Sub-County officially onboarded our ticketing API, assigning dedicated environmental health and road maintenance inspectors to Umoja.

2025 - 2026

Youth Hub & Green Corridor

Opened the free Tena Digital Youth Hub, trained 3,850+ young creators, and planted 4,200 trees along the Moi Drive transit corridor.
