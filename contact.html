Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding issue reporting, volunteer opportunities, and our partnerships.

How long does it take for a reported issue to be inspected?
After submitting a report through our portal, a local ward youth coordinator visits the site to verify and photograph the issue within 4 to 12 hours. The ticket is immediately routed to the Embakasi West Sub-County unit or Kenya Power / Nairobi Water desks. Most standard garbage and drainage issues are addressed within 24 to 72 hours.
Is filing a report or joining cleanups completely free?
Yes, 100% free! Umoja Community Connect is a non-profit civic platform. We never ask for money or facilitation fees to submit reports, attend cleanups, or enroll in our youth skills bootcamps. Any individual soliciting money on our behalf should be reported immediately.
Can I submit a report anonymously?
Yes. While we recommend providing a phone number to receive automated SMS updates when your ticket changes status, you can check the "Submit anonymously" option. Your personal contact details will remain strictly hidden from public view.
How can our court or youth group borrow cleanup tools?
We have a community tool bank located at Tena Social Hall equipped with wheelbarrows, rakes, shovels, gumboots, and heavy-duty gloves. Registered estate court committees can borrow tools free of charge for organized weekend cleanups by contacting our field lead 48 hours in advance.
How do youth apply for the sponsored tech bootcamps?
Simply navigate to our Opportunities Page, choose the program that matches your interests, and click "Apply Now". Shortlisted candidates are invited for an informal orientation and workstation assignment at our digital lab.
