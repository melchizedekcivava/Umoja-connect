Accountability Roadmap

How Your Report Leads to Real Action

Every report logged goes through a structured 4-step verified workflow.

01. Log Issue

Citizen snaps photo with GPS location and submits through our mobile platform.

02. Field Check

Ward youth coordinator inspects the site and verifies urgency within 4-12 hours.

03. County Action

County sanitation, roads, or drainage teams dispatch equipment to resolve the issue.

04. Tree Greening

The cleared area is planted with trees and green seedlings to prevent re-dumping.
